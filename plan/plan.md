# hussh Homepage Redesign

## What it is
A full redesign of the hussh homepage that keeps the brand's dark-and-gold identity and the "shh" mascot.
The page gets a simpler navigation bar, sharper copy, new 3D images, a clearer visual order, and a working email sign-up.

## Who it's for
- People who care about privacy and are deciding whether to try Agent One, hussh's free on-device AI assistant
- Early adopters who want updates on the hardware (Puppy One, Tag One)
- Businesses and partners checking out hussh for their teams

## Core features and experience
- **Navigation bar:** cut from 7 dropdown items to 5 (Agent One, Products, Marketplace, For Business, Company). Blog and Guide move under a new "Learn" item inside Company. The bar keeps search, the account icon, and one main button, "Get Agent One". It turns into a frosted, see-through bar as the page scrolls, and it collapses into a menu drawer on mobile.
- **Visual direction:** the dark, editorial hero and closing sections stay. Warm, light ivory sections sit between them. Gold from the current brand is the only accent color. Headlines use bold display type with plenty of spacing, and layouts lean left and asymmetric rather than centering everything. Interactions get small, subtle animations.
- **Images:** the mascot stays as the main brand character. New 3D images match the current mascot style: a hero image, plus one scene each for Agent One, Puppy One, and Tag One.
- **Copy:** every headline, subhead, and button label is rewritten to be shorter and more direct, while keeping the "sovereign intelligence / owned by you" message.
- **Homepage sections, top to bottom:**
  1. **Hero:** a new headline, the mascot image, two buttons (Get Agent One / hussh Shop), and a trust line (100% on-device · encrypted · free for life)
  2. **Products:** Agent One, Puppy One, and Tag One shown with different-sized cards rather than a uniform grid. Tag One is marked "Coming soon" and has its own early-access sign-up.
  3. **How it works:** three steps, each with a matching sign-in preview
  4. **Marketplace:** "Your info. Your price. Your call." with four steps
  5. **Works with your AI tools:** a dark band showing Gemini, Siri, ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Meta AI, and Copilot
  6. **Closing call to action:** the email sign-up
  7. **Footer:** reorganized with the same link groups, plus the legal and privacy notes
- **Email sign-up:** visitors can enter an email address for "Get Agent One" or for "Tag One early access". Addresses are saved, invalid or duplicate entries get a clear message, and a toast confirms each successful sign-up.

## Phase 1: what gets built now
- The full redesigned homepage, including every section listed above, for desktop and mobile
- The new navigation bar with dropdowns and the mobile drawer
- Four new mascot-style 3D images (hero, Agent One, Puppy One, Tag One)
- Rewritten copy throughout the page
- A working email sign-up for both "Get Agent One" and "Tag One early access", with addresses saved
- Navigation links and other secondary links go to placeholder anchors on the page. Inner pages such as Blog, Shop, and Business are not built in this phase.
- There is no admin view of sign-ups in this phase.
